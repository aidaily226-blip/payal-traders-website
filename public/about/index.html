About Payal Traders

Payal Traders supplies industrial gloves from Delhi, India to buyers at home and abroad. We are a small team, so you deal directly with the owner, Piyush Goel, from your first quote to the final shipment.

What we supply

Our range covers the gloves most workplaces use every day:

  • Grey knitted cotton gloves for packing, warehousing and light assembly
  • Denim jean work gloves for construction, fabrication and material handling
  • Khadi canvas work gloves for general industry and lifting
  • Leather and canvas combination gloves for assembly, logistics and mechanics
  • Split leather and grain leather welding gloves for welding, foundry and high-heat work

Custom sizes, cuff lengths and private labelling are available on request.

How we work with buyers

  • Flexible order sizes. From trial orders to bulk shipments.
  • Free samples for serious buyers. Test the gloves in real work before you commit to a large order.
  • Export documentation handled by us. We prepare the paperwork so your shipment clears smoothly.
  • Same-day replies on WhatsApp. You talk to the person who makes the decisions.

Safety standards

Our professional welding gloves are marked to EN 12477, EN 388 and EN 407. On our blog we explain these standards in plain language, so buyers can check what they are paying for.
